The look of the original app.

- Liquid-Glass style navigation: a glass top bar that appears once content scrolls under it, and a floating glass tab bar with a sliding lens that hides while you read down. Swipe between sections.
- Parliament-blue backdrop with translucent cards in dark mode, iOS-style white cards in light mode.
- Two-tone line icons, chosen from each section's name; collapsible Videos / News blocks with a sunrise mark.
- A light runs round the card while a source is being looked up.
- Android: videos play in the app in a pop-up player that grows out of the middle of the screen; fullscreen turns the phone sideways and back upright.

Downloads: `-android.apk` (Android 8+), `-macos.dmg` (macOS, Apple silicon), `-windows.msi` (Windows 10/11), `-linux.deb`.
The desktop builds are not code-signed yet; see the README for the one-time "open anyway" step. The Android build installs over 1.0.0.
